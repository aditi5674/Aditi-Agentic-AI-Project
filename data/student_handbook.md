# Student Services Handbook — Demonstration Policy

## Tuition Fees and Payment Deadlines
The demonstration fee ledger shows total fees, payments received, pending balance and the currently configured due date. Students should use the official institute payment channel and retain their transaction reference. If a payment is debited but not reflected, they should raise a support case with the transaction reference and payment date. This sample handbook does not create an automatic extension or waive late fees.

## Payment Portal and Technical Problems
For a payment portal error, students should capture the error message, note the date and time, and contact the finance helpdesk. A request for an exception or additional payment time must be reviewed by the finance office. This demonstration handbook does not guarantee that payment on the next working day will be accepted.

## Scholarships and Financial Aid
Scholarship status, scheme name and waiver percentage shown in a student record are illustrative sample data. Renewal depends on the conditions published by the institute and verification by the financial aid office. Missing or unlisted eligibility conditions should be referred to that office rather than inferred by the assistant.

## Academic Records and Programme Guidance
The student profile contains demonstration programme, semester, CGPA and advisor information. Requests for programme changes, course substitutions or changes to official academic records require review by the academic office. The assistant may explain available handbook information but cannot make an official academic change.

## IT Facilities and Student Support
For account access, portal errors or other administrative problems, students should create a support request with a concise description and relevant non-secret reference details. Students must never share passwords, one-time passcodes or API credentials in chat.

## Fee Adjustments and Human Approval
A fee adjustment request is not an approved adjustment. The request is stored with PENDING status and must be reviewed by an authorised human decision-maker in this demonstration workflow. Rejection must not change the outstanding balance. Approval may apply the requested adjustment only after the application verifies that the balance has not changed since the request.
